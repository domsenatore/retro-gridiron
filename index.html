RETRO GRIDIRON

Original arcade football prototype inspired by classic pixel-football games.
YOU 0 - CPU 0
1st & 10

Controls: WASD / arrow keys move the ball carrier. On a pass play, press SPACE to throw to the highlighted receiver. Cross the RIGHT-SIDE goal line to score a touchdown. Tackles advance the down; four failed downs cause a turnover. This is an original prototype and does not use Retro Bowl assets or code.
